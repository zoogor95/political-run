# Balance report: United Kingdom

10,000 computer-vs-computer matches, every seat played by the Hard AI with one strategy's changes. Half are two-player duels covering every pair of strategies in both seat orders; half are free-for-alls of 3 to 5 players. Leaders are drawn at random.

**Pass:** no strategy wins more than 60.0% of its duels against the competitive strategies.

Hoard loses to every other strategy, so it's left out of the field the limit is checked against: beating it flatters a strategy's numbers.

## Strategies

| Strategy | What it does | Duels won vs competitive strategies | Duels won vs all | Free-for-all wins vs even share |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Balanced | The Hard AI as it is: best seats for the money, groups when worth it. | 57.2% | 65.5% | 0.83× |
| Big regions | Concentrates on the regions with the most seats. | 56.7% | 64.8% | 1.16× |
| Small regions | Picks up the cheap regions with few seats that others leave alone. | 38.2% | 49.3% | 1.05× |
| Groups and income | Chases region and national groups for income first, seats later. | 46.4% | 56.7% | 0.69× |
| Rush | Brings regions into contention early to end the campaign while ahead. | 51.5% | 60.9% | 1.34× |
| Hoard | Keeps a quarter of its money back each turn and pours the savings in on the final turn. | (loses to all) | 2.6% | 0.94× |

An even share in a free-for-all is 1.00×: a 4-player strategy at 1.00× wins a quarter of its games.
