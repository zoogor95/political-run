# Balance report: United Kingdom

10,000 computer-vs-computer matches, every seat played by the Hard AI with one strategy's changes. Half are two-player duels covering every pair of strategies in both seat orders; half are free-for-alls of 3 to 5 players. Leaders are drawn at random.

**Pass:** no strategy wins more than 60.0% of its duels against the competitive strategies.

Hoard loses to every other strategy, so it's left out of the field the limit is checked against: beating it flatters a strategy's numbers.

## Match shape

- Length: 8.3 turns on average (5 to 17).
- Second rounds after a tie: 0.4% of matches.
- Matches that hit the 40-turn cap: 0.0%.
- The winner holds 50.0% of the seats on average; 55.9% of winners have a majority.
- Money lost to clashes (two parties aiming for the same step): 31.7% of all spending.
