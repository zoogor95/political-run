# Balance report: Pakistan

2,000 computer-vs-computer matches, every seat played by the Hard AI with one strategy's changes. Half are two-player duels covering every pair of strategies in both seat orders; half are free-for-alls of 3 to 5 players. Leaders are drawn at random.

**Fail:** Big regions win more than 60.0% of their duels against the competitive strategies.

Hoard loses to every other strategy, so it's left out of the field the limit is checked against: beating it flatters a strategy's numbers.

## Strategies

| Strategy | What it does | Duels won vs competitive strategies | Duels won vs all | Free-for-all wins vs even share |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Balanced | The Hard AI as it is: best seats for the money, groups when worth it. | 48.0% | 55.4% | 0.76× |
| Big regions | Concentrates on the regions with the most seats. | 62.1% | 67.0% | 0.74× |
| Small regions | Picks up the cheap regions with few seats that others leave alone. | 39.9% | 48.5% | 0.59× |
| Groups and income | Chases region and national groups for income first, seats later. | 51.2% | 58.7% | 0.60× |
| Rush | Brings regions into contention early to end the campaign while ahead. | 48.9% | 56.3% | 0.76× |
| Hoard | Keeps a quarter of its money back each turn and pours the savings in on the final turn. | (loses to all) | 13.9% | 2.50× |

An even share in a free-for-all is 1.00×: a 4-player strategy at 1.00× wins a quarter of its games.

## Match shape

- Length: 19.7 turns on average (9 to 40).
- Second rounds after a tie: 0.1% of matches.
- Matches that hit the 40-turn cap: 0.9%.
- The winner holds 63.4% of the seats on average; 81.0% of winners have a majority.
- Money lost to clashes (two parties aiming for the same step): 55.5% of all spending.
