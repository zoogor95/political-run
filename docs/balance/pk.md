# Balance report: Pakistan

4,000 computer-vs-computer matches, every seat played by the Hard AI with one strategy's changes. Half are two-player duels covering every pair of strategies in both seat orders; half are free-for-alls of 3 to 5 players. Leaders are drawn at random.

**Pass:** no strategy wins more than 60.0% of its duels against the competitive strategies.

Hoard loses to every other strategy, so it's left out of the field the limit is checked against: beating it flatters a strategy's numbers.

## Strategies

| Strategy | What it does | Duels won vs competitive strategies | Duels won vs all | Free-for-all wins vs even share |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Balanced | The Hard AI as it is: best seats for the money, groups when worth it. | 47.8% | 56.1% | 0.99× |
| Big regions | Concentrates on the regions with the most seats. | 57.4% | 63.8% | 1.06× |
| Small regions | Picks up the cheap regions with few seats that others leave alone. | 46.0% | 53.7% | 0.79× |
| Groups and income | Chases region and national groups for income first, seats later. | 47.7% | 56.3% | 0.98× |
| Rush | Brings regions into contention early to end the campaign while ahead. | 51.1% | 59.0% | 1.03× |
| Hoard | Keeps a quarter of its money back each turn and pours the savings in on the final turn. | (loses to all) | 10.8% | 1.16× |

An even share in a free-for-all is 1.00×: a 4-player strategy at 1.00× wins a quarter of its games.

## Match shape

- Length: 12.8 turns on average (6 to 33).
- Second rounds after a tie: 0.2% of matches.
- Matches that hit the 40-turn cap: 0.0%.
- The winner holds 58.9% of the seats on average; 75.9% of winners have a majority.
- Money lost to clashes (two parties aiming for the same step): 46.9% of all spending.
