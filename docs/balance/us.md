# Balance report: United States

4,000 computer-vs-computer matches, every seat played by the Hard AI with one strategy's changes. Half are two-player duels covering every pair of strategies in both seat orders; half are free-for-alls of 3 to 5 players. Leaders are drawn at random.

**Fail:** Balanced win more than 60.0% of their duels against the competitive strategies.

Small regions loses to every other strategy, so it's left out of the field the limit is checked against: beating it flatters a strategy's numbers.

## Match shape

- Length: 16.6 turns on average (9 to 36).
- Second rounds after a tie: 28.3% of matches.
- Matches that hit the 40-turn cap: 0.0%.
- The winner holds 63.0% of the seats on average; 99.8% of winners have a majority.
- Money lost to clashes (two parties aiming for the same step): 27.5% of all spending.
