# Balance report: United States

4,000 computer-vs-computer matches, every seat played by the Hard AI with one strategy's changes. Half are two-player duels covering every pair of strategies in both seat orders; half are free-for-alls of 3 to 5 players. Leaders are drawn at random.

**Fail:** Balanced win more than 60.0% of their duels against the competitive strategies.

Small regions loses to every other strategy, so it's left out of the field the limit is checked against: beating it flatters a strategy's numbers.

## Strategies

| Strategy | What it does | Duels won vs competitive strategies | Duels won vs all | Free-for-all wins vs even share |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Balanced | The Hard AI as it is: best seats for the money, groups when worth it. | 68.9% | 75.0% | 0.93× |
| Big regions | Concentrates on the regions with the most seats. | 50.2% | 58.8% | 2.86× |
| Small regions | Picks up the cheap regions with few seats that others leave alone. | (loses to all) | 5.4% | 0.05× |
| Groups and income | Chases region and national groups for income first, seats later. | 59.8% | 67.4% | 0.54× |
| Rush | Brings regions into contention early to end the campaign while ahead. | 38.4% | 48.3% | 0.83× |
| Hoard | Keeps a quarter of its money back each turn and pours the savings in on the final turn. | 32.6% | 45.0% | 0.76× |

An even share in a free-for-all is 1.00×: a 4-player strategy at 1.00× wins a quarter of its games.

## Match shape

- Length: 18.6 turns on average (9 to 43).
- Second rounds after a tie: 24.2% of matches.
- Matches that hit the 40-turn cap: 1.9%.
- The winner holds 63.6% of the seats on average; 99.3% of winners have a majority.
- Money lost to clashes (two parties aiming for the same step): 24.7% of all spending.
