# Malaysia pack

Draft numbers for tuning. The Malaysian legal review (design roadmap) and a trademark check on party emblems are still needed before release.

## Seats

- **General seats (222):** the Dewan Rakyat's 222 constituencies by state and federal territory: Sarawak 31, Johor 26, Sabah 25, Perak 24, Selangor 22, Kedah 15, Kelantan 14, Pahang 14, Pulau Pinang 13, Kuala Lumpur 11, Negeri Sembilan 8, Terengganu 8, Melaka 6, Perlis 3, Labuan 1, Putrajaya 1. Each state or territory is one region, so 16 regions.
- **Majority:** 112 of 222. Government formation works as in the other parliamentary packs: the largest party leads, and the next largest join until a majority is reached.
- **No reserved seats.**

## Voters

About 21.1 million registered voters (the 2022 electorate, rounded), spread across the regions by seats. These are estimates, not state rolls: a state's real electorate per seat differs.

## Money

Amounts are in ringgit: base income RM 2.1M a turn for two players, cut 15% for each extra player like every pack. A state's first step costs RM 60K plus RM 6K per seat. Group incomes and national group costs are drafts, to be tuned with the balance simulator (`docs/balance/my.md`).

## Groups

State groups are places: the Northern states, the East Coast, the Klang Valley, the Southern states, Sabah and Sarawak, the Federal Territories, and the West Coast corridor. National groups are kinds of voter by place of living, work or age: urban voters, rural voters, civil servants, young voters, business owners, and factory and estate workers. There are deliberately no groups by race or religion.

## Candidates

Sixteen real politicians with bonuses and penalties from public facts (the seat they hold, the state they govern, their party's 2022 results by region), never from opinion. Notes are short and factual.

## Board

`packages/content/maps/my.json`: Natural Earth state outlines with Sabah and Sarawak moved closer to the peninsula so the board fits the screen. The map is public domain.
