# UK map pack (draft)

**Every number here is a draft sample value for tuning, not final balance.**

- **Regions:** the 12 nations and English regions, with real 2024 seat counts (650 in total, 326 for a majority).
- **Step costs:** each region's first step costs £5,000 + £200 per seat (London: £20,000). Each later step costs 1.1x the previous one.
- **Income:** £250K base money every turn, plus each group you control (£30K to £60K per group, adjusted by your modifier on that group).
- **Groups:** 6 state groups (sets of regions) and 6 national groups. A region can be in more than one group; London and the big cities overlap on purpose.
  - A state group is controlled by leading regions worth more than half of its total campaign cost. Its money can only be spent in its own regions.
  - A national group has its own 15-step meter (first step £20K to £30K, above most regions) and is controlled by leading it with at least 3 steps. Its money can be spent anywhere.
- **Candidates:** 16 real politicians, living and historical. Each modifier is a percent: +10 makes steps in that group's regions 10% cheaper (and will raise that group's income by 10%); negative numbers are penalties. The `notes` field gives the reason shown on the candidate card.
- **AI party names:** comical made-up parties. Never a parody of a real party, religion or community.

Map geometry is not in this pack yet; it comes with the map build tool (M0.3).
